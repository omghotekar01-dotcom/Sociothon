- Analytics accepts explicitly declared interval quantities and canonicalizes water/energy units and timestamps to UTC. Cumulative counter formats are rejected with interval-reading guidance, as specified in the master prompt.
- Electricity analysis uses explicit voltage observations only. Optional CSV voltage requires a V unit; missing or stale voltage is reported as data quality, and persistent out-of-band voltage needs three contiguous readings. Configurable bounds are illustrative, not regulatory thresholds. Zero kWh does not establish an outage; reported-outage context is only a synthetic scenario and is not reconciled with actual utility reports.
- Citizen electricity reports distinguish outage, hazard and voltage-observation categories, may link a same-zone registered feeder, and remain explicitly unverified until staff review. It does not reconstruct historical workflow statuses. Full-analysis water impact/coverage cards remain explicitly labelled outside the time filter. Each domain workspace observation chart and case list now share the zone and absolute time window with URL/navigation persistence, using their own series.
